# Palmyra Launch: ideas to grow and earn (research 2026-10-19)

Sources read: Pump.fun creator-fee and livestream coverage (Decrypt, The Block), Blum Memepad and GasPump coverage (DappRadar, STON.fi blog), Telegram mini-app growth analyses (referral, leaderboards), Telegram Gifts / TON NFT guides, class-action coverage (DLA Piper), Getgems help centre (fees, jetton sales), the TON rebrand notices (Kaiko, KuCoin).
Everything below is an option, not a promise of income. Nothing here is built unless stated.

## Facts that matter
- The native coin was renamed **TON -> GRAM** on 2026-06-15 (community vote, 81.22%); the network keeps the name The Open Network; no swap or migration; anyone asking holders to "migrate" is a scam. The 3-month "(prev. TON)" period has ended.
- Pump.fun raised the creators' share of trading fees tenfold in 2025 and creator income and streaming grew after it. Creators bring the traffic.
- Pump.fun faces class actions alleging its coins were unregistered securities (allegations, status not checked). Operator liability is a real risk: legal advice before heavy promotion.
- Referral systems that reward BOTH sides, leaderboards and daily mechanics are the repeated growth and retention drivers on Telegram mini-apps.
- Getgems: 5% marketplace fee; its fixed-price sale contract (v4) can price in TON or jettons (USDT, NOT listed in its help). No public audit was found for its contracts.

## Can be built now (no new money-moving contract)
1. Trust badges on every coin page: creator fee, free allocation (none), holders concentration, age, fee flows. A differentiator against rug-pull platforms.
2. "About to graduate", "Hot", "New" boards and a live feed (new launch, graduation) posted by the existing English coins channel bot.
3. Share cards (image + deep link with a referral parameter) so launchers and traders can share in one tap.
4. Creator profile and a public "creator earned" figure (read from the chain), to attract launchers.
5. Watchlist and price/graduation alerts through the bot (free, non-custodial), plus a portfolio page for a connected wallet.
6. Languages beyond English/Arabic (Russian, Turkish, Spanish, Chinese) through the existing language switch.
7. Safe-launch presets on the Launch page (fair launch, buy limits) explained in plain words.
8. "Sponsored" slot: a creator pays the owner a flat amount for a labelled featured slot for N days, checked on-chain, no custody.
9. Rename the coin label TON -> GRAM across the platform (labels only, no contract change).

## Needs a decision, legal advice or an audit
- Referral rewards paid from the owner's fee income (both sides): off-chain accounting and payouts; tax and legal questions.
- Omniston routes (STON.fi + DeDust) with a referrer fee, to earn on graduated coins too.
- Own NFT sale flow with a marketplace fee (see TRADING-ROADMAP.md): cannot be tested end to end without wallet signing; an NFT sent to a mis-built sale contract is locked.
- Non-custodial limit orders / DCA; never a bot that holds user keys.
- Points or quests: only badges and status; never promise tokens, airdrops or returns.
