What this is · ما هذه المنصة

An open page where anyone can launch a coin on TON using open-source bonding-curve contracts, and anyone can trade it. We do not hold your funds, custody your wallet, or run the contracts for you: you sign every action in your own wallet.

Fees · الرسوم

Each trade pays a platform fee of 1.5% plus the creator fee chosen by the coin's creator (0% to 2%, shown on the coin). Creating a coin costs about 1 GRAM in network and creation costs plus your optional first buy.

Swaps on the Swap tab are made on the STON.fi DEX. The quote shows the total: the DEX pool fee plus a 0.30% platform fee. Your wallet signs each swap; the platform never holds your funds. Coins that graduated to DeDust are traded there, and the platform takes no fee on DeDust.

Rules for creators · قواعد المُطلِقين

  • No scams, no impersonation of other projects, people or brands, no illegal content, no copied artwork you do not have the right to use, no hateful or sexual content.
  • Do not use the name or symbol of a well-known coin.
  • You are responsible for your coin and for the laws that apply to you.

Invite rewards

If someone joins through your invite link and links their wallet, you are credited 20% of the platform fee (the 1.5%, not their trade) on their buys of platform coins for 180 days. Credits are computed from public on-chain data and shown on the Journey page. Credits are not a salary, a return or a promise: the platform may change or stop the programme, may refuse credits it believes come from self-dealing or abuse, and paying credited rewards out to wallets is not switched on yet.

Moderation · الإشراف

Anyone can report a coin. The platform owner may hide a coin from our pages (it stays on-chain and can still be traded by anyone using the contract directly). Hiding is not a judgement of legality and a report alone never hides anything.

Age and place · العمر والمكان

Use the platform only where it is lawful for you and only if you are an adult. You are responsible for any taxes or regulation that apply to you.
